What is NEXFUND?+

NEXFUND is being developed as a trading evaluation platform with structured challenges and risk criteria.

Are challenges available to purchase?+

Not yet. The listed account sizes and fees are previews; checkout and account activation are not enabled.

Which trading platform will be supported?+

MetaTrader 5 is planned. A live MT5 connection and account provisioning have not been confirmed active.

How can I track my evaluation?+

Use the trader portal to explore the workspace. Live evaluation data requires an active account and backend integration.

Where can I get help?+

Visit the trader guide or open the support area through the trader portal.
